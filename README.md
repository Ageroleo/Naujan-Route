# Naujan Tourism PWA 

An offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to guide tourists and locals through the natural wonders, heritage sites, and communities of **Naujan, Oriental Mindoro, Philippines**.

### ✨ Key Features
- **Interactive Map & Turn-by-Turn Routing:** Real road paths to Naujan Lake, Bancuro Church Ruins, Estrella Falls, and more.
- **Suggested & Alternative Routes:** Verified provincial shortcuts and scenic loop trails.
- **Fare Calculator:** Distance-based fare estimation for tricycles, jeepneys, and Lake Naujan motorized bancas.
- **Offline-First PWA:** Cached destination guides and emergency contacts accessible without cellular reception.
- **Local Directory:** Quick-dial hotlines for MDRRMO, PNP, BFP, and the Municipal Tourism Office.
- **Mascot Integration:** Guided by "Nawi", the Naujan Lake Duck.
